# Hyblis logo

The H monogram with a crosshair (the owner's logo, 2026-11-16).

- `hyblis-logo-original.png`: the image the owner supplied (white on black).
- `hyblis-mark.svg` and `hyblis-mark-black.svg`: the mark as a vector, traced from the original
  (99.4% pixel overlap at 660 px), for light-on-dark and dark-on-light use.
- On the site the mark is the `#logo-mark` symbol in `platform/site/index.html` (filled with
  `currentColor`, so it follows the header colour and turns pink on hover) and the favicon
  `platform/site/assets/logo.svg` (the mark on the dark rounded tile).
